Composition rules
  • Stacked oversized display title on a flat warm brand ground; deliberate colour changes between title lines.
  • Guideline page: left 4/12 explanatory rail and right 8/12 large product or mark specimen; no extra containers.
  • A clearly labelled correct/incorrect pair or a sparse application sheet at consistent scale; keep examples large enough for projection.
  • One warm colour field and a short bold action. Never add a logo, font or illustration that does not belong to this system.
