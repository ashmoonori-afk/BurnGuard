Composition rules
  • Three horizontal ruled bands: period, identity and oversized business title; one small progress marker sequence.
  • Financial update: tightly aligned horizontal result rows, or two equal analytical panels with shared baselines.
  • Two comparable bar charts in paired panels, direct value labels and a shared scale; one current-period accent. Move dense reconciliations to an appendix.
  • Two period/outlook panels and a compact next action; use actual supplied figures only, never invent financial results.
