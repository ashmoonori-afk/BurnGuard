Composition rules
  • Huge two-line title in upper-left 9/12; thin rules divide a shallow lower specimen strip and a narrow image column.
  • Manual spread: label and short instructions occupy left 3/12, one large specimen occupies right 9/12. Use continuous fine rules, no rounded card grid.
  • Show colour as differently sized circular swatches, type as one oversized specimen plus a baseline strip, images as a deliberate contact sheet.
  • Sparse ruled frame with the next action at upper left. Carry the same indexed rail-and-specimen hierarchy into the system overview.
