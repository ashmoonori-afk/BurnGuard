04 / One clear next step →
Composition rules
  • Large title in left 8/12, opposite brand-ground panel in right 4/12 with a gently slanted boundary.
  • Merchant specimen: short explanation in left third and an isolated product or checkout example in the remaining two thirds.
  • Compare actual product applications in a vertical specimen stack; maintain common scale and labels, not decorative dashboards.
  • Two unequal brand-colour planes with one clear action on the larger plane; alternate title, human image and specimen frames.
