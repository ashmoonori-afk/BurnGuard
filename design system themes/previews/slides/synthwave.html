Composition rules
  • Oversized left-aligned type in 8/12; cropped geometric forms occupy the opposite corner or bottom band.
  • Speaker/event content uses one horizontal portrait band with aligned labels. Alternate black, light and brand-accent chapter grounds.
  • Use a 7/5 split: three short statements left, a large example above a caption block right; geometric bands establish hierarchy.
  • One oversized takeaway and one cropped geometric block; never repeat the speaker grid as a default body layout.
