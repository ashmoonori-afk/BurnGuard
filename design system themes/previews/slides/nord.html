Composition rules
  • Quiet title in left 6/12; one analytical line motif in right 6/12, with generous whitespace.
  • Research page: narrow finding column at left 3/12, one large chart at centre 6/12, methodology or comparison in right 3/12.
  • A single readable chart leads; direct labels, units, period and source below. Two charts only when they share a comparison question.
  • A concise finding and a next-step column on a light ground. Printed footnotes become a dedicated appendix slide.
